# Observations

Staged lessons from working sessions, captured per the `observation-log`
skill. `OPEN` entries are provisional — check them before relying on the
Skill, reference, or convention they target, and apply them where they look
sound. A human reviews and promotes them; entries are never silently
deleted, only re-marked.

## Format

```markdown
### obs-YYYY-MM-DD-NN: short title
- status: OPEN | PROMOTED | DISMISSED | STALE
- target: <skill, reference, or area it applies to>
- type: correction | gap | conflict | workaround
- basis: observed | extracted
- observed: <what happened, factual, 1-3 lines>
- evidence: <pointer — file, command, output ref — not the content itself>
- proposed: <the candidate rule or fix, 1-2 lines>
- resolution: <PROMOTED: where it landed / DISMISSED or STALE: why>
```

Type maps to the skill's triggers: correction -> `correction`;
expensive lesson -> `gap`; stale guidance -> `conflict`; repeated
workaround -> `workaround`. `basis` is `extracted` for entries mined from
session history rather than witnessed live. Leave `resolution` unset on
a new entry.
